Our Story

A Home Away
From Home

Karibu — Welcome to Longido Mountain Lodge. Nestled in the vibrant Maasai town of Longido in Northern Tanzania, our lodge sits in the cool shadow of the magnificent 2,637-metre Mount Longido.

Located on the Arusha–Nairobi Highway, just 120km from Arusha, we are perfectly placed for travellers seeking the raw beauty of Tanzania away from the tourist trail. Our lush gardens and tree-shaded grounds create a serene oasis in this Maasai heartland.

Whether you're on a weekend escape, a cultural journey, or an adventure to summit Longido Mountain, our friendly team is here to ensure every moment is memorable.

Activities

Unforgettable Experiences

From thrilling mountain hikes to authentic Maasai cultural immersions — every moment at Longido is an adventure waiting to happen

Adventure

Summit Longido Mountain

Full Day Challenging

Embark on an unforgettable hike to the summit at 2,637m. Trek through montane forest and enjoy spectacular panoramic views of the Rift Valley.

Cultural

Maasai Cultural Walk

Half Day Easy

Walk alongside Maasai warriors through the village. Learn about traditional life, pastoralism, and ancient customs. Visit local homesteads.

Wildlife

Wildlife Safari

Full Day Easy

Explore the Longido Game Controlled Area. Spot elephants, giraffes, zebras, and an abundance of birdlife against stunning backdrops.

Nature

Sunrise Bird Walk

2–3 Hours Easy

Join our expert guide at dawn for magical birding. Longido is home to over 500 bird species, including many endemic species.

Cultural

Maasai Evening Ceremony

Evening Easy

Experience vibrant traditional Maasai singing and jumping dances. A magical cultural evening under the African sky with stories around the fire.

Excursion

Day Trip to Arusha

Full Day Easy

Visit the gateway to Tanzania's famous parks. Explore the cultural heritage centre, Maasai market, and Arusha National Park.
